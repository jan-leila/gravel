Routing Assistant router: a recording routed to it is read as a change to the routing itself ("send anything starting with todo to my tasks app"). The on-device text model rewrites the routing, and the recording waits in the Notebook with what would change until you apply or dismiss it. Tokens and places are hidden from the model.
